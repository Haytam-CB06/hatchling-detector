# Real-footage Studio demo clip

`real_leatherback_demo.mp4` is a 3-second, 10 fps H.264/AAC-compatible demonstration copy derived from the **real** wildlife recording [*Leatherback baby turtle running to sea*](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Leatherback_baby_turtle_running_to_sea.webm) by **Tyler Karaszewski**, licensed [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/). The clip was trimmed, frame-rate converted and H.264 encoded solely for browser preview and a short CPU inference demonstration; no content was generated or replaced. No source audio was present in the copied segment.

The unmodified WebM source and its checksum, creator and license are in `data/external_test/originals/videos/` and `data/external_test/manifests/combined_manifest.csv`. Use the MP4 in the Detection Studio upload area. Preserve the attribution when showing or redistributing it.
